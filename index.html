Common questions

Does Ahead connect to my bank?
No. You add your bills and income yourself. Nothing syncs.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone only. Ahead has no account and no server.

How do I back up my data?
Open Settings, then Export Backup or Export Spreadsheet.

How do I turn on Face ID lock?
Open Settings, then turn on Require Face ID and choose when it locks.

How do I delete everything?
Open Settings, then Erase All Data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.
