Ahead Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Ahead: Money Planner ("Ahead") is made by Michael Boling.

What Ahead collects

Nothing. Ahead does not collect, store, or share any personal information on any server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter, such as bills, income, categories, and balances, is stored only on your device. Ahead does not connect to your bank, and it makes no network requests.

Face ID

If you turn on App Lock, Ahead asks iOS to check Face ID or your passcode. Ahead never sees or stores your biometric data. iOS handles it.

Notifications

If you set reminders, Ahead schedules them on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Exports and backups

If you export a spreadsheet or backup, the file goes where you choose to send it. Ahead does not receive a copy.

Deleting your data

Use Erase All Data in Settings, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, that removes it all.

Children

Ahead collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
